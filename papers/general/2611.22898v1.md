# You Only Encode Once: Sub-Pixel-Shifted Frames for Arbitrary-Scale Remote Sensing Super-Resolution with 2D Gaussian Splatting

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Authors: Canjie Liu, Liuqian Wang, Qi Ming, Wei Lu, Xiaohan Hao, Yuefei Wu, Wentao Chao, Lei Shi
- Link: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22898v1
- Tag: general

Arbitrary-scale super-resolution (ASSR) of remote sensing images remains challenging due to the limited high-frequency information in a single low-resolution (LR) observation. While multi-frame SR can exploit complementary sub-pixel information, existing approaches often require real multi-frame training sequences and repeated feature encoding, increasing data acquisition demands and computational costs. To address these limitations, we propose SPS-GS (Sub-Pixel-Shifted Gaussian Splatting), an encode-once framework that learns from multi-phase LR observations synthesized from individual high-resolution images, without requiring real multi-frame training sequences. Specifically, Phase-Coherent Aliasing Demodulation (PCAD) exploits shift-dependent aliasing through Fourier-based alignment, phase-aware residual fusion, and least-squares aliasing estimation, enabling multi-frame information to be integrated before a single deep encoding pass. Gaussian Projection Alignment (GPA) then iteratively refines Gaussian centers and colors using multi-observation back-projection to promote observation consistency. The refined Gaussians support continuous rendering at arbitrary scales. Extensive experiments on five remote sensing datasets demonstrate strong reconstruction performance against single-image and multi-frame SR baselines, while maintaining competitive inference efficiency at common scales. The code will be publicly released.
