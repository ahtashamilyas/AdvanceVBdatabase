# Accelerating 3D Gaussian Splatting Training through Selective Freezing and Computation Caching

- Date: 2026-11-17
- Authors: He Zhu, Zhiheng Dai, Zihan Liu, Ruyang Li, Hui Wei, Yaqian Zhao, Han Zhao, Xiaofeng Hou, Chen Chen, Jingwen Leng, Yu Feng, Minyi Guo
- Link: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22059v1
- Tag: general

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) becomes a key primitive for immersive 3D experiences. However, its applicability is limited by its long training overhead. Although prior work has shown that gradient accumulation is the dominant bottleneck in training, existing works either have limited speedup or degrade reconstruction quality. To address this issue, we present ElsaGS, an algorithm-hardware co-designed framework that accelerates 3DGS training with negligible accuracy degradation. Our key observation is that most Gaussians converge early, while later iterations only refine a small subset of Gaussians. Thus, we propose a technique, progressive Gaussian freezing, which dynamically freezes well-converged Gaussians and caches their cumulative contributions, thereby reducing computations in both forward rasterization and backward gradient accumulation. To make caching efficient, we further propose an architecture, ElsaGS core, which integrates a pixel-parallel rasterization array with a set-associative frozen Gaussian cache, FGcache. Across three 3DGS datasets, ElsaGS freezes up to 90% of Gaussians with negligible quality loss and achieves up to 20.6x end-to-end speedup and 99.4% energy reduction over GPUs.
