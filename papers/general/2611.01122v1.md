# 3D Gaussian Splatting: A New Paradigm for Wireless Environment Representation

- Date: 2026-10-31
- Authors: Jinya Zhang, Jiajia Guo, Chao-Kai Wen, Shi Jin
- Link: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01122v1
- Tag: general

Wireless channels are shaped by the surrounding propagation environment, making explicit environment representation an effective approach for characterizing channels. By combining explicit environment modeling with learnable radio frequency characteristics, 3D Gaussian splatting (3DGS) emerges as a new paradigm for wireless environment representation. This article establishes a systematic perspective on 3DGS-enabled wireless communications in terms of environment representation. We first clarify the rationale for extending 3DGS from visual scene modeling to wireless communications and formulate a unified framework for wireless environment representation. Building on this framework, we further investigate the applicability of 3DGS-based representations to a broad range of communication and sensing tasks, and characterize key considerations for practical deployment. Finally, emerging directions are highlighted, including cross-scene generalization, near-field channel modeling, real-world data collection, and lifecycle management.
